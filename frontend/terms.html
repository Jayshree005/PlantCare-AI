Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms before using PlantCare AI.

1. Use of the Service

PlantCare AI is designed to help users identify possible plant diseases using uploaded leaf images and provide general plant-care information.

2. AI Prediction

Disease predictions are generated using an AI/ML model and may not always be completely accurate. Results should be treated as guidance and not as a guaranteed diagnosis.

3. Plant Care Recommendations

Prevention and treatment information is provided for general guidance. Users should consider consulting an agricultural expert or plant specialist before using chemical treatments.

4. Uploaded Images

Users should upload clear plant or leaf images to improve prediction results. Users should avoid uploading unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

5. Prediction History

If the user is logged in, prediction results may be stored in their account history so they can view previous plant health analyses.

6. Weather Information

Weather-based information, when available, may be used to provide additional insights about plant health. Weather information may depend on third-party weather data.

7. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for decisions made based on the information provided by PlantCare AI.

8. Changes to the Service

PlantCare AI may be updated or modified as new features, AI models and improvements are added to the system.
