Technology for Healthier Plants

Who We Are

PlantCare AI is an AI-based plant healthcare project designed to help users identify possible plant diseases through leaf images. It aims to make disease information, prevention methods, and plant-care guidance easier to understand and access.

🌍

Our Vision

To make plant disease detection simple, accessible, and useful for everyone.

🎯

Our Mission

To help users identify plant diseases early and understand their symptoms, prevention, and care requirements.
