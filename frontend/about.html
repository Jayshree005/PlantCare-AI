ABOUT PLANTCARE AI

Technology for Healthier Plants

Helping farmers and home gardeners identify plant diseases and make informed plant-care decisions.

🌱

Who We Are

PlantCare AI is an AI-based plant healthcare project designed to help users identify possible possible diseases in supported Aloe Vera and Tomato leaf images. It aims to make disease information, prevention methods, and plant-care guidance easier to understand and access.

🌍

Our Vision

To make plant disease detection simple, accessible, and useful for everyone.

🎯

Our Mission

To help users identify plant diseases early and understand their symptoms, prevention, and care requirements.

OUR PURPOSE

Why PlantCare AI?

Early identification and proper care can help users protect their plants.

OUR FEATURES

What Our Project Offers

🔍

AI Disease Detection

The CNN model analyzes leaf images to predict possible diseases.

🍃

Disease Information

Display general information about symptoms and possible causes for the predicted class.

🛡️

Prevention and Care

Display general plant-care and disease-prevention guidance.

🌦️

Weather-Based Risk

Fetch current weather and provide rule-based plant-care and risk guidance. This is advisory, not a disease diagnosis.

OUR WORKFLOW

How Our AI Works

The current workflow for supported Aloe Vera and Tomato leaf images.

01

Upload Image

The user uploads a clear image of a supported Aloe Vera or Tomato leaf.

02

Image Processing

The image is resized and prepared for model analysis.

03

Plant Validation and Prediction

A plant validator checks the image before the CNN predicts a configured disease class.

04

View Guidance

The user can view the prediction and relevant plant-care information.
